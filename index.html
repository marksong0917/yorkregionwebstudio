Questions

Quick Answers

How much does the service cost in total?

One flat monthly subscription, priced by written quote after a free 15-min call. No setup fee, ever. Your rate is locked while subscribed; bigger scope is quoted in writing too.

Do I own my website and my data?

Yes — the domain, content and data are yours, stored in Canada, exportable any time, and yours to keep if you cancel. We sign an NDA first.

How long does it take?

Your website can be live within days of design approval. Foxuma follows in phases, on a written timeline confirmed before we start.

What do you need from us to start?

A logo, photos, and rough notes on your services — plus your customer list if you want it imported. We handle the rest.
