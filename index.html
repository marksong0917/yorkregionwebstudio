Questions, Answered Straight

Quick answers, no fine print.

How much does a website cost in total?

Starter is $299 to build, then $10/mo for hosting & care. Business is $499 + $39/mo, Growth is $799 + $69/mo. Our first 5 founding clients get 20% off the build. Your payment schedule is confirmed in writing before work starts.

Do I own my website and domain?

Yes — the domain, content, and branding are yours. If you ever leave, we hand over your site files, and the monthly plan cancels with 30 days' notice.

How long does it take?

Most sites go live within 72 hours, starting once we have your content and you've approved the design. Bigger projects get a written timeline before we start.

What do you need from us to start?

Just a logo, any photos, and rough notes on your services. No text ready? We can draft from your Facebook page or existing site — you approve everything before launch.
