Questions, Answered Straight

The things every business owner asks me before starting.

How much does it cost in total?

Starter is $419 in the first year ($299 build + $10/mo), Business is $967 ($499 + $39/mo), and Growth is $1,627 ($799 + $69/mo). Prices are in CAD, HST extra where applicable. With the founding offer (20% off the build fee, first 5 clients), first-year totals drop to $359.20 / $867.20 / $1,467.20. After year one you only pay the monthly hosting & care.

Do I own my website and domain?

Yes. Your domain is yours, and your site content, text, and branding are yours. If you ever decide to leave, I provide your site files so you can move to self-hosting. The monthly plan can be cancelled with 30 days' written notice — you're never locked in. Full details are in my terms, sections 2 and 3.

What do you need from me to start?

Whatever you already have: your logo, any photos, and rough notes about your services and service area. If you have no text ready, I can build the first draft from your Facebook page, your existing website, or your public listings — you approve everything before it goes live.

How long does it really take?

Most sites go live within 72 hours — the 48-hour build and 72-hour go-live clock starts once I have your content and you've approved the design. Bigger or custom projects take longer, and I'll quote that timeline in writing before we start.

Do you write the text and take the photos?

Photos work best when they're really yours, so I use photos you supply (phone photos are fine). For text, send me rough notes in your own words and I'll shape them into clean page copy for your approval — you won't have to write polished marketing text yourself.

Will I rank first on Google?

No one can honestly guarantee a ranking, and I don't promise one. I build every site with the basics Google needs: clear titles and descriptions, mobile-friendly fast pages, and a sitemap. A free Google Business Profile is the biggest single help for local searches, and I'll point you to exactly what to set up.

I already have a website, a domain, or hosting. Can you take over?

Yes — redesigns and takeovers are common. Keep your domain, and I'll rebuild the site and point everything over with no gap in your online presence.

You're one person — what if you're unavailable?

Your site keeps running whether I'm at my desk or not — hosting doesn't depend on me personally. For anything urgent you reach me directly by phone, and because you own your domain and can request your site files at any time, you're never trapped with me.

How do payments work?

Your payment schedule is confirmed in writing before any work starts — no surprises. And per my guarantee, you don't pay the final balance until you've approved the design.

What does the monthly fee actually cover?

Hosting, SSL, security updates, and your included content edits — plus backups on Business and Growth. Each package lists exactly what's included, and you can cancel with 30 days' notice.
