Questions

Quick Answers

How much does the service cost in total?

One plan for the Foxuma Hub: $99/mo including the first user, +$19/mo per user for users 2–10, +$9/mo per user after the tenth — every account counts as a user, and everything is included: all features, updates and the Fox AI assistant. Our first 5 founding customers pay $199/mo flat for up to 10 users, locked for 2 years. The Estimator and Production modules are quoted per customer — a setup fee, no monthly price. Prices in CAD, per company. Cancel anytime — unused days are refunded pro-rated over 30 days.

Do I own my website and my data?

Yes — the domain, content and data are yours, stored in Canada, exportable any time, and yours to keep if you cancel. We sign an NDA with every client.

What is Foxuma?

Foxuma is an all-in-one work hub: leads, jobs, production, schedules, team chat, email and reports in one place, with a built-in assistant throughout. Your website's forms feed straight into it.

How long does it take to get set up?

Your website can be live within days of design approval. Foxuma follows in phases, on a written timeline confirmed before we start.

How does the online estimator work?

Our estimator template is loaded with your prices, materials and options. Customers price their own job on your website in minutes — and the lead lands in your Foxuma with their selections attached.

Hosted, or on our own machine?

Your choice, inside your written quote: hosted for ease, or installed on your own machine. Either way your data stays in Canada and belongs to you.

What happens to my data if I cancel?

Your data remains yours at all times, including after cancellation. You can export it at any time, and when you cancel you receive a complete copy of your database. We keep the hosted copy and its backups for 90 days after your cancellation date, with a reminder before the 90 days are up, then delete them permanently — sooner if you ask, confirmed in writing.

Foxuma vs. a spreadsheet — why switch?

Most shops run on a spreadsheet, a group chat, an inbox and memory — leads get missed, follow-ups fall through, and job status lives in one person's head. Newood Millwork replaced its master spreadsheet and Monday with Foxuma: 138 projects now run across leads, production, calendar, team chat and email in one system.
