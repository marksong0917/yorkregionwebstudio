Questions, Answered Straight

Quick answers, no fine print.

How much does the service cost in total?

One setup fee, then one monthly subscription. Core is $399/mo with a $1,500 setup. Signature is $699/mo with setup from $2,500. Full Office is $999/mo with setup from $5,000. Pay annually and two months are free. Your exact setup fee is quoted in writing before work starts.

Do I own my website and my data?

Yes — the domain, content, branding, and business data are yours. Your data is stored in Canada, you can export it at any time, and if you ever cancel you keep your complete database. We sign an NDA before onboarding, and subscriptions cancel with 30 days' notice.

How long does it take?

Your website can be live within days once we have your content and design approval. The CRM and customized features are set up in phases over the following weeks, with your timeline confirmed in writing before we start.

What do you need from us to start?

A logo, any photos, rough notes on your services, and your current customer or job list if you want it imported. We handle the rest — you approve everything before launch.
