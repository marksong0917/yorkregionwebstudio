Questions, Answered Straight

Quick answers, no fine print.

How much does the service cost in total?

Every project is priced by quote. After a free 15-min call, you get one written price — a one-time setup plus one flat monthly subscription covering the Work Hub, your website, the built-in assistant, and your estimator. Founding clients (our first 5) get the setup fee waived, and your quoted rate is locked while you stay subscribed. Bigger scope — bespoke features, multiple locations, an online store — is quoted in the same process, also in writing.

Do I own my website and my data?

Yes — the domain, content, branding, and business data are yours. Your data is stored in Canada, you can export it at any time, and if you ever cancel you keep your complete database. We sign an NDA before onboarding, and subscriptions cancel with 30 days' notice.

How long does it take?

Your website can be live within days once we have your content and design approval. The Work Hub and your customized features are set up in phases over the following weeks, with your timeline confirmed in writing before we start.

What do you need from us to start?

A logo, any photos, rough notes on your services, and your current customer or job list if you want it imported. We handle the rest — you approve everything before launch.
