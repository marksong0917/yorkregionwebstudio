Questions

Before you ask

How much does a website cost?

Every site is quoted in writing after a short call. The price depends on the pages, the features and the content — you approve the quote before we start, and there are no surprise add-ons.

How long does it take?

Most sites launch in two to four weeks once we have your content. The timeline is confirmed in your written quote.

What do you need from me?

Your logo if you have one, your words and photos — or we help you create them. One short call at the start, one review round before launch.

Do you look after the site after launch?

Yes. Hosting, domain, updates, backups and small changes are handled by us, so the site stays fast and current.

Can you rebuild my current site?

Yes — we redesign and rebuild existing sites, and move your content over. Your domain and email stay yours and keep working.

Do I own my website?

Yes. The domain, the content and the design files are yours. We sign an NDA with every client, and everything is stored in Canada.
