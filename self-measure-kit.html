How It Works

Six short steps, on the customer's phone

01

Pick the space

Kitchen, closet or another room — the wizard adjusts to what they picked.

02

Measure wall by wall

Room size in centimetres, then each wall with its doors, windows and obstacles marked.

03

Style and photos

They choose a finish level and can add photos of the space as it is now.

04

You get the summary

Dimensions, total cabinet run, obstacles, finish level and a price range from your rates — in your inbox, ready to quote from.

Questions

Before you ask

How much does the Self-Measure Kit cost?

It is added to your website package and priced in your written quote, like everything we build — you approve the price before we start, and there are no surprise add-ons.

Are customer measurements accurate enough to quote from?

Accurate enough for a solid first quote. The wizard walks the customer wall by wall in centimetres and flags doors, windows and obstacles, so the summary is structured — not a guess typed into a contact form. You still take a final field measurement before production, the way you always would.

Does it use my prices or yours?

Yours. Your per-metre rates and finish levels sit in one settings block in the kit. The range a customer sees is worked out from your numbers, not ours.

Where do the summaries go?

To your email inbox — or straight into Foxuma as a new lead with the summary attached, if your shop runs on the work hub.

Do I need a new website?

No. The kit is added to your current website, or built into a new one if we are making it. Either way it carries your branding.
