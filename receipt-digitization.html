Questions

Before you ask

What does receipt digitization cost?

Clearing a backlog is a one-time job from $199 in CAD — the final price is quoted in writing once we see how much paper there is. Keeping new receipts filed as they come is $29 a month.

How accurate is it?

Pages are read by OCR at about a second a page, and then every document’s vendor, date and total are verified against the record it belongs to before it is filed. Anything that cannot be read clearly is flagged to you, never guessed.

What happens to my original paper?

Your call — originals are returned to you, or kept as you direct. The digital archive is stored in your own cloud storage, under your account.

Can you file receipts against my books?

Yes — with our bookkeeping service, each filed receipt is matched to its transaction in QuickBooks Online, so the paper and the books agree.
