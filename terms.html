York Region Web Studio provides one service: a premium website together with the Newood Work Hub business management application and customized features built for the client's business (for example, an online estimator). The service includes design, build, setup, data import, configuration, training, hosting management, security updates, and support, as described in the client's written quote. Deliverables and timelines begin once all necessary client assets (logos, images, and text copy) are delivered. Before onboarding, the studio and the client sign a mutual non-disclosure agreement (NDA) covering the client's business data and the studio's methods.

2. Subscription & Billing

The service is billed as a subscription, monthly or annual. Annual subscriptions are priced lower than monthly — two months free each year. Your plan, setup fee, and subscription rate are confirmed in writing before work starts, and your rate is locked for as long as you stay subscribed. Your written quote is valid for 30 days from the date on it. Founding offer: our first five clients pay no setup fee, and their subscription rate is locked for two years. Subscriptions may be cancelled with 30 days written notice. On cancellation, the walk-away terms in Section 3 apply.

If a payment fails. We email you and retry the card. You have 14 days to update your payment method. If payment has still not gone through after 14 days, your subscription pauses: your team cannot sign in to the Work Hub and support pauses until payment is made. Billing pauses too — you are not charged for the paused time. Your website stays online and your data is not touched. If the payment is not fixed within 60 days, the subscription is treated as cancelled, and the cancellation terms in this section and Section 3 apply.

Support. Standard support is included with every subscription: email support answered by the next business day, software updates, and nightly backup checks. Optional add-ons — Priority ($149/month) and Full Care ($299/month) — add same-business-day response by phone and remote fixes; Full Care also includes a monthly system health check and a quarterly on-site visit in the GTA. Add-ons are billed monthly. Add-ons start as soon as you add them, and the first month is prorated to your regular billing date. If you remove an add-on, it stays active until the end of the month you have already paid for, and you are not charged for it again. There are no refunds for partial months.

Quoted in writing. Every engagement is priced for the client's business. Your exact setup fee and subscription rate are confirmed in writing after a consultation, before any work starts.

Customized features. Your responsibilities. You are responsible for the accuracy of prices, rates, and business information you supply, and for keeping login credentials for your systems secure.

4. Your Data

Your business data is yours: your customers, your leads, your job history. It is stored in Canada, you can export it at any time, and if you ever cancel, you keep a complete copy of your database. We sign an NDA with every client, and we never share or sell your data.

After 90 days, we delete the hosted copy and its backups permanently. You can ask us to delete everything sooner at any time — just ask, and we will confirm the deletion in writing.
