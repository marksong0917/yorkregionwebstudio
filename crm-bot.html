Most shops run on a spreadsheet, a group chat, an inbox and memory. Leads get missed, follow-ups fall through, and job status lives in one person's head. Foxuma replaces all of it — one system, configured around how your business works.

One flow, start to finish: an enquiry lands from your website or estimator, becomes a project in one click, moves through production on a live board, and lands on the calendar — with your team chat, email and files on the same record. Nothing is typed twice.

Dashboard & My Day — your day at a glance: new leads, jobs waiting, upcoming installs, and what needs your attention.

Leads & Customers — every enquiry in one pipeline, with follow-up reminders and duplicates flagged before they split your history.

Projects & Production — one page per job — stage, schedule, files, photos, tasks — and a production board the whole team can see.

Calendar & Scheduling — measures, deliveries and installs on one team calendar, each event visible to the right audience.

Team Chat — rooms and direct messages inside the system, with @mentions and voice messages. Work talk stays at work.

Your Email, in Foxuma — your own mailbox, worked inside Foxuma: threaded replies, drafts, labels, undo-send. One click turns an email into a lead, a ticket or a task. Nobody else can see your mail.

Tickets, Requests & Forms — service calls and deficiencies as tickets with owners and due dates; time-off, purchase and expense requests with one-click approvals; measure sheets and checklists that file themselves against the job.

Inventory, Assets & Library — materials and equipment registers, maintenance dates that surface before they are overdue, and your SOPs and guides in one library.

Reports, Search & Activity — owner reports across the business, one search box for everything, and a full activity log of who changed what.

The right view for each person: the Owner sees everything, including open balances. Managers and the office run the day-to-day without the money screens. Sales sees only its own leads. The shop floor sees production, calendar and forms. If a role doesn't have a module, it doesn't appear — permission is the menu.

The built-in assistant works from your live records, under the same permissions as the person asking — in English or Chinese. Ask it a question and it answers from your real data. Tell it to change something and it shows you the exact change first; nothing is written until you confirm, and every change lands in the activity log. It runs on your own server — your data never leaves it.

Your data is stored in Canada and covered by a signed NDA. Foxuma runs in your browser — no downloads, nothing to install — with automatic backups every night. One flat monthly fee covers your whole team: unlimited users, no per-seat charges, and your rate is locked while you stay subscribed. If you ever cancel, you keep your complete database.

Configured around your business, not the other way round: your stages, fields, checklists and forms are set up as your templates before your team logs in. Your estimator is configured with your prices. Start with the modules you need today and switch more on as you grow — a feature built from scratch is scoped and quoted in writing.

Standard support is included: next-business-day email support, software updates, nightly backup checks. Priority ($149/month) adds same-business-day response by phone and remote fixes. Full Care ($299/month) adds a monthly system health check and a quarterly on-site visit (GTA). Add or remove them any time.

Running live today at Newood Millwork in Concord: 138 projects, the whole team, one system — it replaced their master spreadsheet and their Monday board. Most shops are live in 1 to 2 weeks once we have your data, on a timeline confirmed in writing.
