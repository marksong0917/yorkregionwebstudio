Privacy Policy

Effective Date: September 5, 2026 · Updated: October 24, 2026

1. PIPEDA Compliance Overview

York Region Web Studio complies with the Personal Information Protection and Electronic Documents Act (PIPEDA) of Canada. We respect your privacy and are committed to safeguarding personal information collected through our website.

2. Information Collection & Purpose

We collect personal information (name, email address, phone number, and project details) submitted voluntarily via our contact forms. This data is strictly used to evaluate project requests, respond to inquiries, and deliver the requested service.

We count visits to this website anonymously — no cookies, no personal information, and no tracking across other sites.

3. Form Processing & Third-Party Disclosure

Contact form submissions are securely processed through the studio's own Hub system over encrypted HTTPS channels. We do not sell, rent, trade, or share your personal information with third-party advertisers or external marketing lists.

4. Data Security & Access Rights

We maintain appropriate administrative and technical safeguards to protect your personal data against unauthorized access. For service clients, the studio hosts your Work Hub and business data: it is stored in Canada and covered by a signed non-disclosure agreement (NDA). The studio accesses client systems only to provide setup, support, and maintenance under the client's plan. Under PIPEDA, you have the right to request access to or deletion of your personal contact records at any time.
