Questions

Before you ask

What does the Website Care Plan cover?

Hosting on our own always-on servers, scheduled backups, software updates, uptime monitoring and small content edits — for $49 a month, in CAD. Bigger changes are quoted in writing first.

My site was built by someone else. Can you still take care of it?

Usually, yes. We look at the site first, tell you plainly what shape it is in, and confirm the move before anything changes. If it needs work before it can be cared for properly, that work is quoted in writing.

What counts as a small edit?

Text changes, hours, prices, photos, a new team member, a seasonal banner — the everyday updates a small business site needs. New pages and redesigns are bigger work and are quoted separately.

Who do I contact when something is wrong?

Us, by email. No hosting-company ticket queues. And because the site is monitored, we often know about a problem before you do.
