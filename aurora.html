How much does a website cost for an Aurora small business?

Starter is $419 in the first year ($299 build + $10/mo hosting & care), Business is $967 ($499 + $39/mo), and Growth is $1,627 ($799 + $69/mo). Prices are in CAD, HST extra where applicable. My founding offer takes 20% off the build fee for my first 5 clients.

How fast can my site go live?

Most sites go live within 72 hours once I have your content and you've approved the design — that's when the build clock starts. If you have no text or photos ready, I can build the first draft from your Facebook page or existing website.

Are you actually based in Aurora?

Yes — I'm Mark Song, a developer based in Aurora. You deal with me directly: no agency, no call centre, no account manager. I also serve Newmarket, Richmond Hill, Vaughan, Markham and across York Region.

Do I own my website and domain?

Yes. Your domain and your site content are yours. If you ever leave, I provide your site files, and the monthly plan can be cancelled with 30 days' written notice.

What guarantee do I get?

You approve the design before the site goes live, I revise it until you're happy with how it looks, and you don't pay the final balance until you approve it. Every site also includes a 30-day post-launch warranty: display bugs and form errors fixed free.

Will I show up on Google?

I build every site with the basics Google needs — clear titles and descriptions, mobile-friendly fast pages, and a sitemap — but no one can honestly guarantee a specific ranking, and I don't promise one. A free Google Business Profile is the biggest help for Aurora local searches.
