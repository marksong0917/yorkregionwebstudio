Questions

Before you ask

What is Foxuma?

Foxuma is a work hub — one system where a shop runs its day: leads and customers, jobs and production, schedules, team chat, invoicing and payroll hours. It replaces the pile of spreadsheets, group chats and paper notes most shops run on.

Do I need a York Region Web Studio website to use Foxuma?

No. Foxuma works on its own. But if we built your website, the two plug straight into each other — quote requests, estimator results and measure-kit summaries from your site land in Foxuma as new leads, with nothing re-typed.

Who sets it up?

We do. Your customers, jobs and team are loaded in, the system is shaped around how your shop actually works, and your people are shown how to use it. You do not get a login and a manual — you get a running system.

How much does Foxuma cost?

Foxuma’s own plans start at $99 a month, listed at foxuma.com. If you want our studio to set it up for your shop — customers and jobs loaded in, your team onboarded and trained — that setup work is priced in your written quote, and you approve the price before we start.

Is my business data private?

Yes. Your customers, prices and jobs are yours alone — never sold, never shared, never used for anyone else's business. Each shop's data is kept separate from every other shop's.
