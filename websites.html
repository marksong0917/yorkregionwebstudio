Questions

Before you ask

How much does a website cost?

A standard business site runs $899 to $1,499 depending on pages and features — website-in-a-week pricing, confirmed in a written quote before we start. Looking after it afterwards is the Website Care Plan at $49 a month: hosting on our own servers, backups, software updates, uptime monitoring and small content edits included.

How long does it take?

Most standard sites launch in about a week once we have your content. The timeline is confirmed in your written quote.

What is the Google business profile setup?

We create or clean up your Google business profile — categories, services, hours, photos — so it matches your website exactly and customers find the right details. It costs $149 on its own and is free with any new website build.

Can you rebuild my current site?

Yes — we redesign and rebuild existing sites and move your content over. Your domain and email stay yours and keep working. The care plan can also take over a site built elsewhere, after a look at what shape it is in.

Do I own my website?

Yes. The domain, the content and the design files are yours. We sign an NDA with every client, and everything is stored in Canada.
