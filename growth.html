Questions

Before you ask

What does the Growth Package include?

Four things for $249 a month in CAD: a reviews engine that requests and follows up a Google review after every job, quote chasing that follows up your open quotes until they close, after-hours answering for missed calls and website messages with estimates booked, and social media upkeep — your job photos polished, 8 posts a month written and scheduled, and your Google business profile kept current.

How does the lead generation add-on work?

On top of the package, we can run lead generation for your trade and area on commission: $50 to $150 for each booked appointment, with the exact rate agreed in writing before we start. No appointment, no fee.

What are job videos?

Short before/after videos cut from the photos and clips of a finished job, made for your social feeds and website. They are $39 each for non-members.

Do you need my passwords?

You stay the owner of every account. Access is set up properly, with your permission, and can be removed by you at any time.

Is there a contract?

The package is month to month. The monthly note shows what it did; if it stops paying for itself, you stop it.
