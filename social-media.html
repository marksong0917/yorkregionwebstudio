Questions

Before you ask

What does social media upkeep cover?

Your job-site photos polished, 8 posts a month written and scheduled across your profiles, and your Google business profile kept current — for $199 a month in CAD. You send photos as you take them; everything else is handled.

I barely take photos. Is this still worth it?

A few photos per job is plenty — one finished kitchen is a week of posts once it is polished and written up properly. Part of the setup is a simple habit for grabbing them on site.

Do you need my passwords?

You stay the owner of every account. Access is set up properly, with your permission, and can be removed by you at any time.

Who writes the posts?

The same studio team that runs social for Newood Millwork — a working cabinet shop here in the GTA. The posts are about your real jobs, in plain words a customer understands.
