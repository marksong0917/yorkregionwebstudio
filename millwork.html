Questions

Before you ask

What does Foxuma cost, and what do you charge?

Foxuma’s own plans start at $99 a month, listed at foxuma.com. Our studio’s setup — your customers and jobs loaded in, your team onboarded and trained — is priced in your written quote, and you approve the price before we start.

What is the branded estimator?

A cost estimator that lives on your own website under your own branding, the pattern built for Newood Millwork’s site: visitors price their own kitchen or closet job and send you the details. It is $49 a month per shop, hosted on our fleet.

How does remote estimating work?

You send the drawings and measures for a job; the estimating and takeoff work is done remotely and returned to you, priced per job at $69 to $169 depending on size — the price is confirmed before we start.

Who is behind this?

York Region Web Studio works out of Newood Millwork’s shop in Concord. The offers on this page exist because that shop needed them first — they run in production there every day.
