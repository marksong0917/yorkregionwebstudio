Questions

Before you ask

Who is this service for?

Trades and small businesses in York Region and the GTA with roughly 1 to 15 staff who run QuickBooks Online — cabinet and millwork shops, contractors, installers and similar businesses whose books and pay runs need a steady monthly hand.

What do the plans cover?

Starter ($99/mo) covers bank feed posting and categorization for up to about 75 transactions a month, receipts and invoices filed to a cloud archive, HST-ready records and a plain monthly summary. Standard ($199/mo) covers up to about 200 transactions and folds in payroll prep (hours in, pay-ready sheets out, holiday and stat pay figures prepared) and the digitized receipt archive, plus invoices created from your POs, an accounts-receivable follow-up list and a month-end close with an accountant-ready package. There is no setup fee; bigger shops get a custom quote.

Can we work in 普通话？

Yes — the whole service is available in English or 普通话 at no extra cost: summaries, questions and paperwork in the language you prefer. 账目和工资的事，用中文沟通完全没问题。

Do you file my taxes or run my payroll?

No. This is bookkeeping and preparation only — you run the actual pay run, and tax filing and year-end stay with your own accountant. What we hand them is a clean, complete set of records, so their work is faster and cheaper.

I have a backlog of paper receipts. Can you clear it?

Yes — paper backlog cleanup starts from $149 one-time. Your receipts and invoices are read, verified and filed into a sorted, searchable cloud archive; the final price is quoted in writing once we see the size of the pile.

Who does the work?

Your books are handled by Tina, the operator who runs Newood Millwork’s books end to end — the same hands that post that shop’s bank feed, file its receipts, prep its payroll and raise its invoices every month.
