Every Month

What gets done

Bank feed posted

Every transaction posted and categorized, with rules for the charges that repeat — so the feed stays at zero instead of piling up.

Receipts filed

Receipts and supplier invoices filed to a cloud archive against the right transaction. When your accountant or the CRA asks, the paper is there.

HST-ready records

The tax detail is captured as the work is posted, so HST time is a report, not a rebuild.

Payroll prep

Hours in, pay-ready sheets out. You run the pay run with clean numbers in front of you.

Invoices from POs

Purchase orders become proper invoices, matched to the job — no re-typing, no missed extras.

A plain monthly summary

What came in, what went out, who still owes you — in plain words, every month, whether you ask or not.

Questions

Before you ask

Who is this bookkeeping service for?

Trades and small businesses in York Region and the GTA with roughly 1 to 15 staff who run QuickBooks Online — cabinet and millwork shops, contractors, installers and similar businesses whose books need a steady monthly hand.

What do the plans cover?

Starter ($149/mo) covers bank feed posting and categorization for up to about 75 transactions a month, receipts and invoices filed to a cloud archive, HST-ready records and a plain monthly summary. Standard ($249/mo) covers up to about 200 transactions and adds payroll prep, invoices created from your POs, an accounts-receivable follow-up list and a month-end close with an accountant-ready package. There is no setup fee; bigger shops get a custom quote.

Do you file my taxes?

No. This is bookkeeping and preparation only — tax filing and year-end stay with your own accountant. What we hand them is a clean, complete set of records, so their work is faster and cheaper.

How do my receipts get to you?

However they come now — photos, email, or a shared folder. Every receipt and invoice is filed into a cloud archive against the right transaction, so nothing lives in a shoebox or a glove compartment when your accountant or the CRA asks.

Who does the work?

Your books are handled by Tina, the operator who runs Newood Millwork’s books end to end — the same hands that post that shop’s bank feed, file its receipts, prep its payroll and raise its invoices every month.
