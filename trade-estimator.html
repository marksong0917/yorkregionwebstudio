The Estimator — a customized feature of the service.

Get Your Free Quote

Our proven estimator template, loaded with your prices, your materials and your options. Customers price their own job on your website — and the lead lands in your Work Hub. The first real price a customer gets usually wins the job: a guess over the phone sounds like a guess, and a quote that takes three days loses to one that takes three minutes.

Built for your trade: kitchen remodeling, cabinetry & millwork, closets & storage, bathroom renovation, countertops, flooring, decking & fencing, basement finishing, painting, interlocking and more — priced from your rates, by size, material and options.

See one working: Newood Millwork's kitchen estimator (estimator.newoodmillwork.com). Customers price a full kitchen in about two minutes — instant result first, contact details after — built on that shop's real prices and finishes.

How it works: a free 15-min call and your price list; we configure the template around your trade and your numbers; you approve it; it goes live on your website. Most go live in 1 to 3 weeks. Every finished quote lands in your Work Hub as a lead, with the customer's selections attached.

The estimator is a customized feature of the Signature service — not a separate product, and there is no separate estimator bill. Configuring it with your prices is included in your written quote; send updated prices any time and we apply them under your plan. It calculates from your prices, so the range matches how you quote — you still confirm the final quote after a site visit, same as you do now.
