How much does the estimator feature cost?

The estimator is a customized feature of the service, included on the Signature plan ($699/mo). Building it is scoped inside your one-time setup fee ($2,500–$5,000 depending on your materials, options and math). There is no separate estimator bill. How accurate is the estimate it gives?

It calculates from your prices, your materials and your options, so the range matches how you quote. It is a ballpark for the customer — you still confirm the final quote after a site visit, same as you do now.

How long does it take to build?

Most estimators go live in 1 to 3 weeks, starting once we have your price list. You approve it before it goes on your website, and its leads land in your CRM from day one.

What do you need from us to start?

Your price list, your materials and finish options, your logo, and your service area. No price list written down? We build one with you on the call.
