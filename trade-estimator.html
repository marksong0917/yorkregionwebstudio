Custom Pricing

Every Trade Is Different. So Is Every Estimator.

A flooring estimator is not a kitchen estimator — so there is no fixed price tag. Builds are priced as a range, and your exact price is quoted in writing before any work starts.

The Range

Build: $1,500 – $5,000 CAD. Most projects land between $2,200 and $3,500.

Hosting & care: $99 – $199/mo. Hosting, lead emails, price updates, and support included.

Your price depends on how many materials and options you offer, how detailed the math is, and extras like a printable PDF quote.

One extra job usually covers the build. A kitchen, a deck, or a whole-home flooring job is worth far more than the estimator that brought it in.

How much does a Trade Estimator cost?

Every estimator is custom, so builds range from $1,500 to $5,000 CAD — most land between $2,200 and $3,500. Hosting, lead emails, price updates and support run $99 to $199/mo. Your exact price is quoted in writing after a free 15-min call.

How accurate is the estimate it gives?

It calculates from your prices, your materials and your options, so the range matches how you quote. It is a ballpark for the customer — you still confirm the final quote after a site visit, same as you do now.

How long does it take to build?

Most estimators go live in 1 to 3 weeks, starting once we have your price list. You approve it before it goes on your website.

What do you need from us to start?

Your price list, your materials and finish options, your logo, and your service area. No price list written down? We build one with you on the call.
